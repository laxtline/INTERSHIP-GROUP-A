About the Project

Smart WiFi-Controlled RGB LED Lighting

We built a smart addressable RGB LED lighting system controlled over WiFi. An ESP32 microcontroller running WLED drives 10 meters of WS2811B LED strips, letting you change colors, effects, and brightness right from your phone.

💡

LED Setup

  • Lights: 10 meters of WS2811B RGB LED strips
  • Controller: ESP32
  • Power: 12V and 5V adapters with a common ground (GND) connected between the 12V supply, the LEDs, and the ESP32
  • Connections: LED VCC → 12V supply; LED DIN → pin D22 on the ESP32
📶
